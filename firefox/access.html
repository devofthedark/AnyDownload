Access to websites

AnyDownload downloads straight from the sites media is on, which are often not the page you're on, e.g. YouTube's videos come from googlevideo.com. Firefox only lets it do that, and read a site's cookies for Use Cookies, once you allow it to access websites. It only does so when you download something.

AnyDownload can access websites. You can close this tab and download again.

Firefox didn't allow it. You can also turn on "Access your data for all websites" in AnyDownload's Permissions tab in about:addons.
