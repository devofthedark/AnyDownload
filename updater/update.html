Finish the update

  1. Unzip from your downloads folder.
  2. Open AnyDownload's details in your browser's extensions, which show the folder it's Loaded from.
  3. Replace everything in that folder with the files inside the unzipped folder. manifest.json has to end up directly in AnyDownload's folder.
  4. Come back here and reload AnyDownload.

Reloading stops any AnyDownload panels that are open, along with their downloads. Close them with ✕ and click the AnyDownload button again to get a working one.

If your browser says it couldn't load the extension, check that manifest.json is directly in AnyDownload's folder, then click Retry.

Instead of replacing the files, you can also remove AnyDownload on the extensions page, then click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. You'll have to accept the terms of use again.

Checking for updates

AnyDownload asks GitHub (api.github.com) for its latest release about twice a day. Like any request, that shows GitHub your IP address; nothing about you or your downloads is sent. Pre-releases are never offered.
