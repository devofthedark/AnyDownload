Thanks for installing AnyDownload

AnyDownload runs yt-dlp entirely inside your browser, so you can save videos and audio from the pages you visit without installing anything else.

Before you start, please read and accept the terms of use.

How to use it

  1. Open the page with the video you want to save.
  2. Click the AnyDownload icon in your browser toolbar. A small panel opens in the corner of the page.
  3. Wait for the panel to say Ready to download. The first time can take a few seconds while yt-dlp loads.
  4. Click Download. The finished file is saved to your usual downloads folder.

Tips

  • Click Load available formats to choose a specific video quality, audio track or output file type. Leaving everything on the defaults picks the best quality.
  • Changing the output file type may mean re-encoding, which is slower and can lower the quality.
  • Drag the panel by its title bar to move it, and click ✕ to close it.
  • If you need to sign in to watch a video, sign in on the site first. AnyDownload uses your browser's session for that site.
